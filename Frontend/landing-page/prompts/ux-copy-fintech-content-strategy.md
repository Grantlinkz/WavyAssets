# Implementation Prompt: Plain-Language Fintech UX Copy & Content Strategy

## Objectives & Role
As Lead UX Writer and Fintech Content Strategist, transform all customer-facing copy across WavyAssets from dense institutional jargon into clear, plain English at an 8th-grade reading level. Eliminate buzzwords like "terminal", "leverage", "paradigm", "synergy", "disrupt", and "algorithmic execution engine". Weave high-intent SEO keywords into headers and metadata, while providing full deliverables for every asset vertical and the platform dashboard.

---

## High-Intent SEO Keywords Strategy
- **AI-driven Investments**: `smart automated investing`, `AI investment funds`, `hands-free investing`
- **Stock Trading & Portfolios**: `invest in stocks online`, `stock portfolio app`, `buy pre-IPO shares`
- **Car Inventory & Financing**: `auto investment platform`, `browse & invest in cars`, `classic car financing`
- **Digital Wallets & Cash Management**: `digital money wallet`, `multi-currency cash account`, `secure money storage`
- **Crypto Investments**: `secure crypto vault`, `crypto savings account`, `simple crypto investing`
- **Real Estate**: `real estate investing online`, `commercial property shares`, `rental income payouts`
- **VIP Cards**: `metal debit card`, `asset backed card`, `zero foreign fee card`

---

## Vocabulary Rules & Conversions

| Old Institutional Term | Plain English Replacement | Tone & Clarity Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trading Terminal / Command Deck** | **Dashboard** or **Trade Screen** | Familiar, friendly, and instantly understandable |
| **Automated AI Execution / Algorithmic Engine** | **Smart Rules** or **Hands-free Investing** | Focuses on user outcome, removes fear of black-box algorithms |
| **Liquidity Pool / Capital Allocation** | **Available Balance** or **Investment Amount** | Clear personal finance phrasing |
| **Automotive Securitization / Freeport Depository** | **Browse & Invest in Cars** | Clear verb-driven action |
| **Sovereign Wealth Enclave** | **Secure Vault Account** | Grounded and transparent security language |
| **MPC Quorum Attestation** | **Multi-Key Security** / **Two-Step Approval** | Easy to grasp security mechanism |
| **Syndicate Feed** | **Live Market Rates** | Standard financial naming |
| **Delta-Neutral Basis Arbitrage** | **Low-Risk Market Strategies** | Direct, honest, non-intimidating |
